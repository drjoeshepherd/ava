# Ava Visual Canon v1.0

**Status: LOCKED**  
**Primary visual authority:** `Ava v1.0 — Canonical Character Construction Sheet.png`

## Design statement

Ava is a compact, stylized, ageless, non-human machine character. Her silhouette is immediately readable through an oversized head, compact torso, short limbs, rounded mechanical forms, silver-white bob, circular listening modules, and a central chest light. She must not drift into a teenage or adult humanoid with robot accessories.

## Locked proportions and silhouette

- Approximately **2.24 heads tall** in the canonical neutral standing pose; the measured front-view ratio is **2.235668790**.
- Oversized head as the primary silhouette and expression mass.
- Compact torso with short, sturdy limbs.
- Rounded, balanced forms rather than a slender humanoid figure.
- Compact mechanical hands and feet.
- No long arms, long legs, narrow fashion silhouette, exaggerated waist, or adult human proportions.
- Ageless and non-sexualized in every view, pose, and costume decision.

The final construction sheet's turnaround is the authority for front, three-quarter, side, three-quarter-back, and back views. Do not use proportions labeled approximately 3.5 heads from earlier images.

### Measurement clarification

The original ~2.5-head description was an approximate design characterization. Direct measurement of the locked canonical raster establishes Ava's canonical front-view ratio at approximately 2.24 heads tall (2.235668790). Raster-derived geometry measurements supersede earlier approximate numeric descriptions.

## Head, face, and eyes

- Large, expressive face integrated into a clearly non-human design.
- Large amber eyes with depth and a controlled reflective finish.
- Eye shape supports attention, curiosity, thought, concern, and delight without makeup styling.
- No realistic human skin, pores, cosmetics, eyeliner, lipstick, fashion lashes, or glamour treatment.
- Facial design remains restrained; expression is led by eyes, lids, brows, and small mouth shapes.

## Hair treatment

- Short silver-white bob.
- Hair reads as a designed flexible shell or fiber form organized into broad sculpted groups.
- Soft stylized grouping, clear silhouette, and controlled secondary motion.
- No realistic strand-by-strand simulation, loose flyaway realism, salon styling, fashion hair, or long hairstyle.
- Hair must not obscure the listening modules or destabilize the head silhouette.

## Materials and construction

- **Primary exterior:** white ceramic/polymer shell; clean, premium, softly reflective, and manufactured rather than biological.
- **Mechanical structure:** graphite joints, hands, interfaces, and internal structural elements.
- **Supporting accents:** restrained neutral gray only where construction clarity requires it.
- **Emissive accent:** amber/gold only.
- Minimal industrial detailing with smooth, understandable construction.
- No exposed random wiring, ornamental circuitry, battle damage, cyberpunk greebling, or excessive mechanical complexity.

## Signature listening modules

- Circular modules sit at the sides of the head and are essential to Ava's silhouette.
- Dark graphite housing integrates cleanly with the hair and head structure.
- Each module has an independently controllable amber/gold emission ring.
- The modules communicate attention and listening through intensity, rhythm, and directed motion patterns, never through hue changes.
- They are not headphones, ears, animal features, or decorative jewelry.

## Central chest emitter

- A centered circular light is integrated into the upper torso.
- The emitter is a primary emotional and connection signal, not a reactor, weapon, superhero emblem, or power-level indicator.
- It uses amber/gold emission only.
- Its animation may pulse, expand, soften, concentrate, or briefly synchronize with the listening rings.

## Emotional light language

Amber/gold is Ava's **only** emotional light color. Gold means attention, connection, curiosity, and discovery.

Emotion is expressed through **brightness, rhythm, location, and motion**, not through color changes. No RGB system, mood colors, blue thinking light, red warning state, rainbow effects, or multicolor status display.

All light behavior should preserve material readability. Emission supports acting; it does not wash out the face or replace performance.

## Canonical states

| State | Eyes and pose | Listening modules | Chest emitter | Timing/quality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Idle** | Calm, present, neutral | Very low ambient glow | Soft resting glow | Stable, almost imperceptible |
| **Listening** | Eyes orient first; attentive stillness | Brighten or pulse with controlled activity | Quiet support | Responsive, measured |
| **Curious** | Slight widen; attention gathers | Brighter, subtly active | Gentle answering glow | Open, exploratory |
| **Thinking** | Focused gaze; small compression or pause | Slow, steady patterned motion | Low steady pulse | Deliberate, not busy |
| **Connection** | Softened eyes; open posture | Warm coordinated glow | Soft expanding pulse | Gentle, synchronized |
| **Discovery** | Eyes brighten/widen; small lift | Brief energetic increase | Clear answering pulse | Momentary spark, then settle |
| **Delight** | Small or broad smile; subtle cheek raise | Warm bloom with restrained liveliness | Warm pulse/bloom | Joyful, never frantic |
| **Concern** | Attentive narrowing or brow change | Dimmer, tighter, more contained | Reduced and concentrated | Quiet, careful |
| **Focus** | Directed gaze; controlled stillness | Narrow, precise emission pattern | Steady low support | Concentrated, minimal |

## Explicit anti-drift rules

Do not introduce teenage or adult humanoid proportions, realistic human skin, realistic hairstyles, makeup, fashion-model styling, sexualization, long humanoid limbs, excessive mechanical complexity, cyberpunk styling, RGB or multicolor emotional lighting, or canine anatomy or behavior.

When a reference conflicts with this document or the canonical construction sheet, treat the conflicting feature as rejected development material.
