# AVA-ANIM-001 — Butterfly

**Status: Canonical animation brief**  
**Approximate duration:** 7 seconds  
**Dialogue:** None

## Purpose

Demonstrate Ava's canonical attention hierarchy, restraint, light language, subtle head tilt, gentle hand performance, stillness, and sense of discovery.

The butterfly is a visual motif for curiosity, emergence, and discovery. It is not a pet or permanent sidekick.

## Setting

A clean, tactile, understandable space with a quiet neutral background. The scene should feel slightly magical through the butterfly's presence, not through generic science-fiction effects. No blue holograms, Matrix code, glowing brain imagery, or decorative circuitry.

## Beat sheet

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| **0.0–0.8 s** | Ava is mostly still in a relaxed neutral pose. Eyes are calm. Listening rings and chest light hold a soft idle amber glow. |
| **0.8–1.5 s** | A small glowing golden butterfly enters Ava's peripheral vision. Ava's **eyes track first**; the head and torso remain still for a beat. |
| **1.5–2.2 s** | The listening ring nearest the butterfly brightens with a restrained attentive response. The opposite ring may answer subtly. Ava's head follows the eyes with a small, asymmetric curious tilt. |
| **2.2–3.3 s** | Ava slowly raises one hand. The torso participates only enough to support the reach. Fingers open gently and settle into a stable landing shape. |
| **3.3–4.2 s** | The butterfly circles once and lands on one finger. Ava becomes briefly still. The stillness is the emotional center of the shot. |
| **4.2–5.1 s** | Ava's eyes widen slightly. The chest gives one soft amber connection pulse. A tiny smile appears; optional subtle cheek raise. No broad grin. |
| **5.1–6.0 s** | The butterfly lifts away. Ava does not chase it with her body. Her eyes follow first while the hand remains quietly suspended. |
| **6.0–7.0 s** | The head follows the butterfly with a small delayed turn. Listening and chest light settle toward idle. The hand begins a controlled return or holds at the final frame, depending on edit needs. |

## Performance notes

- Acting order: eyes → light response → head → torso → hand/body.
- Preserve meaningful pauses; do not fill the shot with idle motion.
- The head tilt is small and asymmetric.
- The reach is careful, open, and unhurried.
- Butterfly motion is light and readable, not frenetic.
- Ava's delight is contained: slightly widened eyes, one soft chest pulse, and a tiny smile.
- Hair follows the head in broad sculpted groups with subtle delayed settle.
- All emotional emission is amber/gold only.
- Keep Ava at locked approximately 2.5-head proportions with compact limbs.

## Camera and staging

- Favor a medium or medium-close composition that includes Ava's face, listening modules, chest emitter, raised hand, and butterfly.
- Keep the butterfly's path readable against the background.
- Avoid aggressive camera moves. A locked camera or nearly imperceptible push is preferred.
- Do not let bloom obscure the eye line, finger contact, or chest pulse.

## Sound

No dialogue. Optional sound design may include a restrained mechanical eye/head movement, a soft listening-ring tone, a gentle chest pulse, and delicate butterfly movement. Avoid cute pet sounds or magical excess.

## Acceptance criteria

- Eyes clearly move before the head.
- The nearest listening ring responds before the head turn.
- Ava uses a subtle asymmetric tilt.
- The hand rises slowly with articulated, restrained fingers.
- The butterfly clearly lands on one finger.
- Stillness is held after contact.
- The chest produces one soft connection pulse.
- The smile remains small.
- On departure, eyes lead and head follows.
- No dialogue, frantic cartoon motion, canine behavior, multicolor light, or off-model proportion drift.
