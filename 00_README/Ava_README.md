# Ava Source of Truth

This directory is the durable source of truth for Ava, the recurring animated face of Joe Shepherd's work on AI, agents, technology, and the future of work.

## Canon status

**Ava v1.0 is LOCKED.** Do not redesign, reinterpret, modernize, or “improve” v1.0. Production work must preserve the approved character, proportions, materials, emotional-light system, acting language, and authority boundaries documented here.

Any intentional change requires an explicitly approved new version, such as **v1.1** for a compatible refinement or **v2.0** for a material redesign. Never overwrite v1.0 to incorporate a change.

## Authoritative files

1. `Ava v1.0 — Canonical Character Construction Sheet.png` — primary visual authority; the final approximately 2.5-head construction sheet.
2. `01_Canon/Ava_Character_Bible_v1.0.md` — purpose, personality, relationships, trust rules, story grammar, and prohibited behaviors.
3. `01_Canon/Ava_Visual_Canon_v1.0.md` — locked visual design, materials, proportions, and emotional-light states.
4. `02_Production/Ava_Production_Spec_v1.0.md` — modeling, topology, rigging, controls, exports, and naming.
5. `02_Production/Ava_3D_Production_Handoff_v1.0.md` — self-contained Blender/modeling/rigging handoff.
6. `03_Animation/Ava_Animation_Principles_v1.0.md` — performance, motion, facial, hand, and hair rules.
7. `03_Animation/AVA-ANIM-001_Butterfly.md` — canonical first animation brief.

If text and an image appear to conflict, use the final canonical construction sheet together with the written v1.0 canon. Stop and request clarification rather than inventing a compromise.

## Change control

Before changing Ava:

1. Identify the proposed change and why it is needed.
2. Confirm whether it is compatible with v1.0 or requires a major revision.
3. Obtain Joe's explicit approval.
4. Create newly versioned files; do not silently edit the meaning of locked v1.0 files.
5. Record the decision, date, affected assets, and migration implications.

External production, publishing, and commitments remain subject to Joe's specific authorization.
